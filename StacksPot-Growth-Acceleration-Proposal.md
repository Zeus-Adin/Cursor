## 1. Objective

Accelerate Stacks user adoption through sponsored pot campaigns that boost rewards, deepen STX activity, and introduce more participants to Bitcoin-secured DeFi on Stacks.

---

## 2. Executive Summary

StacksPot is a decentralized, yield-powered community pot protocol on Stacks. Users join transparent reward pools while preserving principal and earning Bitcoin-secured DeFi yield.

Early growth is constrained less by product readiness than by **reward perception**—users weigh expected upside against commitment size and wait time. To close that gap, StacksPot requests a **500,000 STX** Endowment sponsorship allocation for a **5-month Growth Accelerator**.

Sponsorship capital is **delegated into pots each 2-week cycle**, then **returned to the sponsor address** when the cycle closes. The **same 500,000 STX** is redeployed across all **10 cycles**. The Endowment’s capital ask does not multiply with cycle count.

| Scope | Cycles | Pots | Capital Required |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Per cycle | 1 | 23 | 500,000 STX (delegated) |
| Per month | 2 | 46 | 500,000 STX (same capital, reused) |
| **5-month program** | **10** | **230** | **500,000 STX (same capital, reused)** |

---

## 3. How Sponsorship Capital Works

1. Endowment allocates **500,000 STX** to the sponsor address.  
2. That STX is delegated across the **23 pots** in the active cycle.  
3. When the cycle ends, delegated amounts are **returned to the sponsor address**.  
4. The **same STX** is redeployed for the next cycle.  
5. This repeats for **10 cycles** over 5 months.

One allocation powers the full campaign without requiring new capital each cycle.
